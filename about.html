About

I'm Nabeel Mumtaz. I ship what I design.

Full Stack Software Engineer & UX/UI Designer based in St. Louis. I don't hand designs over a wall — I design what I can ship, then engineer it end to end. Five projects, one person: that's the portfolio, and that's the point.

The story

Founder first, by habit.

Return It is my main company: doorstep return pickups, live in St. Louis — a driver collects your returns and handles the rest. I founded it, designed the iOS app in Figma, and I'm engineering the whole platform myself.

Alongside it, four side projects: FleetFuel, a native iOS app turning fleet fuel cards into automatic cashback; RishtaAunty, a safety-first Muslim AI matchmaking app; RepTracker, an iOS + Apple Watch app that validates gym reps for gift-card rewards; and Parallel, a chat app that runs your tasks in parallel. Same formula — designed and engineered by a team of one.

In 2026 I added formal design credentials to a full-stack engineering background, earning the Google UX Design Professional Certificate — and immediately putting it to work on real products with real users, not classroom exercises.

Background

Different lenses, better products.

B.A. Psychology (Illinois Wesleyan, 2014), M.S. Human Nutrition (Columbia, 2015), then medical-school coursework at the American University of Antigua before leaving to build software full-time. Psychology taught me why users behave; nutrition taught me systems thinking — what goes in shapes what comes out.

Five years running office operations at a psychiatry practice (2019–2024): EHR systems, HIPAA-compliant records, workflow automation. Plus five-plus years tutoring students in math, science, and test prep. I design for people who need to trust the product — and for students who need it to just work.
