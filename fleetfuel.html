FleetFuel. Automatic cashback on every fill-up.

A native iOS app for fleet drivers and fleet companies that makes fuel rewards passive: connect the company fuel card once through secure tokenization, and every fuel purchase is automatically detected, tracked, and eligible for real-time cashback. Built in Swift and SwiftUI by a team of one.

The problem

Fuel is a top fleet expense — and rewards are broken.

Fuel is one of the largest operating expenses for fleet operations: delivery networks, logistics companies, owner-operators, and commercial trucking all spend significant revenue on gas every month. But today's fuel rewards are fragmented and demand active participation — drivers sign up for loyalty programs, remember the right card at the right station, and manually track rewards.

Worse, most station loyalty programs offer minimal rewards, while fleet card networks (WEX, Comdata, Fuelman) that could offer better rates keep that margin for themselves instead of passing savings to drivers. What fleets actually want: passive rewards with zero workflow friction, plus real visibility into where they're overpaying.

The approach

Connect once. Earn automatically.

FleetFuel is built around one principle: the driver should never think about rewards again. The company fuel card is connected once through secure card tokenization. From that point forward, every fuel purchase is automatically detected, tracked, and eligible for cashback credited in real time.

But cashback alone isn't the product — clarity is. The app turns raw fuel transactions into answers: where the money goes, whether you're overpaying, and which nearby station would have been cheaper. Passive earning, active insight.

The business model funds the rewards. FleetFuel partners with fuel card networks and gas station chains — who gain customer data, transaction volume, and loyalty — and those partners fund the cashback, not the driver. FleetFuel's value is being the interface that drives customer behavior.

Key features

Everything a fleet driver needs, nothing they have to do.

⛽ Automatic cashbackRewards on every fuel transaction with no extra step — credited instantly, funded by network partners.
🧾 Transaction feedEvery purchase logged with date, station, gallons, price per gallon, and total. Search and filter by station.
📊 Spending dashboardFuel spend across days, weeks, and months — budget performance and period-over-period trends, clearly visualized.
💡 Savings recommendationsCheaper nearby stations surfaced automatically, based on historical fill-up patterns.
📈 Real-time insightsSpending trends, average price per gallon, and flagged anomalies to optimize fueling strategy.

Roadmap

Start with fleets, expand from there.

  • Phase 2 — real integrations with WEX, Comdata, and Fuelman for live transaction feeds In progress
  • Phase 3 and beyond — expansion to gig economy drivers (rideshare, delivery) with adapted features for personal fuel cards and consumer-focused rewards Planned

Why it works

Five reasons the model holds.

1. Real painFleets actively want to cut fuel costs and see their spending — the demand is already there.
2. Passive by designConnect once, earn automatically. No behavior change required from drivers.
3. Sound unit economicsPartners fund rewards out of higher volume and data — not out of the driver's pocket.
4. ScalableStart with one fuel card network and expand; the same model extends to gig and consumer drivers.
5. DefensibleHigh switching costs once the primary fuel card is integrated into the workflow.
