How I work

Founder, designer, and engineer — in that order, all at once.

I don't hand designs over a wall — I design what I can ship. Every project here started as a real business problem, got designed around the feeling the brand needs, and is being engineered by the same person who drew the screens.

That constraint is the advantage: pricing models, backend states, and empty states are designed together, so nothing in the UI is decorative fiction. Ask me about taking a 1,000+ test suite from 87.1% to 99.79% pass rate, writing 140 patent application drafts for my own startup, or designing in Figma and shipping the product as a team of one.

The background behind the work is cross-disciplinary: a B.A. in Psychology and an M.S. in Human Nutrition, medical-school coursework before software, five years running office operations at a psychiatry practice (EHR systems, HIPAA-compliant records), and five-plus years tutoring students in math and science. Different lenses, better products — more on the About page.
