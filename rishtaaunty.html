The problem

Muslim matchmaking is proven — and broken.

The incumbents run on swiping volume: more swipes, more paid likes, more revenue. That model is fundamentally at odds with what marriage-minded Muslims actually want — family involvement, deen alignment, and above all, safety. Diaspora Muslims get apps that either ignore their values or tokenize them.

Meanwhile the real experts — the rishta aunties with 10+ years of matchmaking wisdom — were never invited into the product. RishtaAunty puts one inside the app.

The approach

An AI aunty who actually aunties.

Aunty isn't a chatbot skin — she's the product's operating system. She interviews you conversationally (not a form) about values, family, deen, timeline, and non-negotiables, then turns it into a structured profile you review. Every day she proposes 3–5 curated matches with specific, honest explanations — including the differences, not just the alignments.

And she controls every introduction. You can browse profiles, but only Aunty introduces — through double opt-in, with your note screened first ("Beta, 'sup' is not a rishta"). Declines are silent. Every message is screened pre-delivery. Safety isn't a feature here; it's the architecture.

The design language is the rose — sacred in Islamic tradition, culturally inclusive, unmistakably about marriage rather than casual swiping. Deep crimson and ivory, serif headings, warm unfiltered photography, and micro-interactions in Aunty's voice: rose petals when she says yes, gentle reflection when it's a no.

Key features

Guardianship, end to end.

🎤 Conversational onboardingAunty asks deep, open-ended questions and builds your structured profile from the conversation — values, family, deen, timeline, deal-breakers.
💌 Daily curated proposals3–5 matches a day with research-backed compatibility analysis across 10 dimensions — explained in Aunty's voice, differences included.
🤝 Aunty-guarded introductionsDouble opt-in only. Your note gets screened before it's passed on with her honest take. Silent declines — nobody gets rejected to their face.
🛡️ Pre-delivery message screeningAI screens every message for safety and respectfulness — blocks the pushy, the inappropriate, and the premature "add me on WhatsApp."
👪 Family modeParents or walis can talk to Aunty for general updates — never chat contents. The family is included without being surveilled.
📊 Transparent moderationVerification, behavior-tracked limits for new accounts, one-tap reporting with women's reports prioritized, monthly moderation stats published.

Why it works

Five reasons the model holds.

1. Real, underserved painMarriage-minded Muslims want values-aligned matching with family involvement — nobody builds for them properly.
2. Safety as architectureDouble opt-in, screened messages, silent declines, verified identities — trust is structural, not a settings page.
3. Aligned revenuePremium features enhance safety and confidence — the core experience stays free, unlike swipe-volume monetization.
4. Density strategyCity-by-city launch with a waitlist beats the cold-start problem that kills most dating apps.
5. Absurdly leanTwo-weekend MVP at ~$10–25/month. The AI does the work of a matchmaking team.
